Privacy policy

Trace Education app. Last updated 4 October 2026.

Who we are

Trace Education ("the app") is provided by Brandon Pieters for Yavneh College, Hillside Avenue, Borehamwood WD6 1HL. Yavneh College is the data controller for the pupil and parent information shown in the app.

Who can use the app

Only parents and carers recorded by Yavneh College as having parental responsibility for a pupil currently at the school. The school can remove access for any account, for example where a court order is in place.

What the app shows you

Information the school already holds about your child: timetable, behaviour points, attendance marks, report card entries and praise certificates. You only ever see information about your own children.

What the app collects

  • Your email address, to check it matches the school's records and to send you sign-in codes.
  • A notification token for your device, if you allow notifications, so the app can tell you about new behaviour points, praise certificates and report card entries.
  • Basic security records, such as when sign-in codes are requested, to protect accounts.

We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking, and we do not sell or share your data.

Notifications

Notifications are optional. You can turn them off at any time in your device's settings. They are only sent between 7am and 9pm.

Where data is stored

Data is stored securely by the school's systems provider, Supabase, on servers in the UK. Sign-in emails are sent through an email delivery service, and notifications are delivered by Apple.

How long we keep it

Your app account and notification tokens are kept while your child is at Yavneh College and deleted after they leave. Sign-in security records are kept for up to one day.

Your rights

You can ask the school to see, correct or delete your information, or to stop your app access, by contacting the school office on 020 8736 5580. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).

Contact

Questions about this policy: bpieters@yavnehcollege.org
