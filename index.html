Privacy policy

Trace Education

Support for parents and carers of pupils at Yavneh College, Borehamwood.

What the app does

Trace Education shows you your child's timetable, behaviour points, attendance, report card entries and praise certificates, and can notify you when something new is recorded.

Signing in

Enter the email address the school has on record for you. We'll email you a 6-digit code to type into the app. If no code arrives, check your junk folder, then make sure you used the same email address the school uses to contact you.

The app is only for parents and carers the school has recorded as having parental responsibility for a pupil currently at Yavneh College.

Something looks wrong

The app shows the school's own records. If information about your child looks incorrect, please contact the school office rather than the app.
