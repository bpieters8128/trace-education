Privacy policy

Trace Staff app. Last updated 15 October 2026.

Who we are

Trace Staff ("the app") is provided by Brandon Pieters for Yavneh College, Hillside Avenue, Borehamwood WD6 1HL. Yavneh College is the data controller for the staff, pupil and parent information used in the app, and the school's own data protection policies apply to it.

Who can use the app

Only current Yavneh College staff, signing in with their school Microsoft account. What each person can see and do follows their role in Trace, exactly as on the Trace website. The school can remove access at any time.

What the app uses

  • Your name, school email address and role, to sign you in and show you the right information.
  • The records you create or update through the app, such as behaviour logs, report card entries, lesson observations, notes, photos or documents you attach, and emails you send to parents. These are stored in Trace as school records.
  • A notification token for your device, if you allow notifications, so the app can tell you about cover, report cards, parent replies, staff notices and alerts you have set up.
  • Basic security and diagnostic records, such as sign-in times, to keep Trace secure and working.

Face ID is handled entirely by your iPhone: the app is only told whether unlocking succeeded and never receives your face data. The camera is only used when you choose to, for example to read a pupil's name or attach a photo.

We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking, and we do not sell or share your data.

Pupil information

Pupil information shown in the app comes from the school's own records and is only shown to staff whose role allows it. Pupil photos are not shown in the app. The app does not store pupil records on your phone beyond what is needed to show the current screen and a short-lived list for searching.

Where data is stored

Data is stored securely by the school's systems provider, Supabase, on servers in the UK. Emails are sent through the school's Microsoft 365 account, and notifications are delivered by Apple.

How long we keep it

School records created through the app are kept in line with the school's records retention policy. Your device's notification token is removed when you sign out or leave the school.

Your rights

You can ask the school to see, correct or delete your information under data protection law by contacting the school office on 020 8736 5580. You also have the right to complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk).
