Trace Staff

Support for staff at Yavneh College, Borehamwood.

What the app does

Trace Staff is the phone version of Trace, the school's own staff system. It shows your timetable and cover for the day, lets you look up pupils you work with, log behaviour, fill in report cards, email a pupil's parents and read staff notices. It can notify you about cover, report cards that are due, replies from parents and alerts you have set up.

Signing in

Tap Continue with Microsoft and sign in with your school Microsoft account, the same one you use for email. You can then unlock the app with Face ID. The app locks itself after a few minutes in the background.

The app is only for current Yavneh College staff. Access follows your role in Trace, so you see the same information as you do on the Trace website.

Something looks wrong

The app shows the school's own records. If information about a pupil looks incorrect, please raise it in school in the usual way. For problems with the app itself, contact us below.
